What We Do

End-to-end geospatial intelligence.
From raw pixels to boardroom-ready decisions.

01

InSAR Displacement Monitoring

Millimetric ground movement detection using Persistent Scatterer (PS) and Small Baseline Subset (SBAS) interferometry. Sentinel-1 C-band, NISAR L-band, TerraSAR-X.

mm precision 12-day repeat
02

Rainfall & Hydrometeorological Analysis

ERA5-Land hourly, CHIRPS daily, GPM IMERG. Intensity-Duration threshold calibration and automated WATCH / WARNING / CRITICAL alerts for rainfall-triggered landslides.

hourly data global coverage
03

Vegetation & Change Detection

Sentinel-2 multispectral analysis. NDVI, EVI, NDMI, BSI time series. Pre and post-event damage assessment at regional to continental scales.

10m resolution 5-day repeat
04

Machine Learning Risk Classification

Isolation Forest anomaly detection, K-Means clustering, LSTM forecasting, Bayesian uncertainty quantification. Site-calibrated models with explainable outputs.

Python reproducible
05

Geo-Digital Twin Platform

Integrated monitoring dashboards fusing InSAR, rainfall, vegetation & geotechnical data. Real-time APIs, SMS/email alerts, interactive web dashboards.

cloud-native API access
06

Geotechnical Support & Reporting

SPT interpretation, hydraulic conductivity estimation, failure mechanism analysis, slope stability back-analysis. Technical reports for your engineering team.

How We Work

From orbit to decision
in four steps.

01

Acquire

Satellite data streams in automatically from Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, NISAR, ERA5 and CHIRPS via Google Earth Engine.

02

Process

InSAR displacement, vegetation indices, rainfall accumulations computed through reproducible Python pipelines.

03

Interpret

Machine learning classifies risk. Bayesian uncertainty quantifies confidence. Thresholds trigger alerts.

04

Alert

Automated alerts via SMS & email. Monthly reports. Interactive dashboards. Board-ready PDFs.

About GeoClaw

We think like engineers.
We act like scientists.
We deliver like a startup.

GeoClaw is a geospatial intelligence consultancy specialising in satellite-based ground monitoring. We combine InSAR technology, optical imagery, hydrometeorological data and machine learning to deliver actionable risk intelligence for infrastructure, slopes and communities.

Our team has backgrounds in InSAR processing, geotechnical engineering, remote sensing and machine learning. We publish our methods. We open-source our code. We explain our findings in plain language — because decisions depend on trust, not jargon.

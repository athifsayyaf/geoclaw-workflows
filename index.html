InSAR Displacement Monitoring

  • LOS velocity maps

Rainfall & Climate Analysis

  • Hourly rainfall tracking
  • I-D threshold analysis

  • Automated WATCH/WARNING/CRITICAL alerts

Vegetation & Change Detection

Our Technology

How It Works

From satellite to alert in four steps — no ground equipment required.

01

Data Acquisition

SAR imagery (Sentinel-1), optical imagery (Sentinel-2), and meteorological data (ERA5, CHIRPS) are automatically collected for your area of interest.

02

Processing & Analysis

InSAR displacement maps, vegetation indices, and rainfall accumulations are computed using our cloud-based processing pipeline. Machine learning classifies risk levels.

03

Geo-Digital Twin

All data layers are fused into an integrated digital twin of your site — displacement trends, rainfall patterns, vegetation health, and risk scores on a single dashboard.

04

Alerts & Decisions

When thresholds are crossed, automated alerts go to your team via SMS and email. Monthly reports and on-demand analysis keep stakeholders informed.

2. **Fill-residual soil interface** = most likely slip surface
3. **SP13 anomaly** (20m to rock) = buried valley acting as groundwater collector

5. **Variable rock depth** (1-20m) = irregular drainage creating localized pore pressure buildup
6. **Lateritic residual soils** can have high permeability when dry but swell and lose strength when saturated

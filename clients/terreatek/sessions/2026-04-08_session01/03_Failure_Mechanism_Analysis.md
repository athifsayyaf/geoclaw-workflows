## 3. Failure Type Classification

### Most Likely: Translational Slide (Rainfall-Triggered)

| Parameter | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Failure Type | Translational slide along fill-residual soil interface |
| Material | Fill material (silty clay/sandy clay, N=2-5) |
| Slip Surface | Fill-residual soil contact (sub-horizontal to parallel with slope) |
| Trigger | Intense rainfall causing perched water table |
| Depth | 2-5m (thickness of fill layer) |
| Movement Rate | Likely rapid once initiated |
| Volume | Moderate (depends on affected area) |

### Alternative/Contributing Mechanisms

1. **Debris flow** - if initial slide remobilized with high water content
2. **Rotational failure** - possible at steeper sections or where fill is deepest
3. **Differential settlement** - due to variable rock depth (SP13 vs SP04)
4. **Piping/internal erosion** - water flowing along fill-residual interface
